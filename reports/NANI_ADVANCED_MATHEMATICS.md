# NANI Advanced Mathematics

## Implemented in Phase 2 scaffolding
- `sequential.rs`: cost-adjusted information gain, stopping criteria, value of information.
- `hardware.rs`: hardware tier classification and adaptive sampling.
- `recovery_memory.rs`: verified outcome tracking and empirical success rates.

## Investigated but not fully implemented
- Hierarchical Bayesian models, correlated evidence, survival analysis, distributionally robust optimization.
- These require real-world data not yet collected.

## Current baseline
- Deterministic rules and Bayesian diagnosis remain the decision makers.
- Advanced models are experimental pending validation.
